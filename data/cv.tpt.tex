    \textit{Research Summary:} Moore’s law scaling is rapidly slowing, galvanizing the search for alternative architectures or technologies to complement the weaker aspects of CMOS.  My research focuses on identifying these emergent weaknesses in conventional CMOS and corresponding out-of-the-box solutions to share the computational burden.  While it is unlikely that CMOS will ever be completely replaced due to its maturity and ubiquity, in applications where general-purpose computing is unsuitable -- whether due to resource constraints or high throughput requirements -- unconventional advances like those I have explored in my research will be necessary.
